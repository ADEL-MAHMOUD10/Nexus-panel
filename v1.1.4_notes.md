# Nexus Core v1.1.4 Release Notes

### 🛡️ Unified Executive Obsidian Confirmation System (Zero Browser Says Dialogs)
- **Native Browser Confirm Annihilation**: Completely removed all browser-native `window.confirm(...)` and alert popups (`"nexus.sigmacode.online says"`), replacing them with an integrated executive modal design system.
- **`useConfirm` Hook & `ConfirmContext`**:
  - Promise-based asynchronous dialog flow (`const ok = await confirm(...)`).
  - Obsidian Black glassmorphism design language (`bg-[#0c0d14]/95 backdrop-blur-2xl border-white/[0.12]`) matching the rest of the Nexus executive UI.
  - Glowing status accents & custom themed icons for:
    - 🔴 **Danger**: Single & Batch Client deletions, Inbound listener removal.
    - 🟡 **Warning**: Inbound traffic resets, Client usage resets, Bulk traffic clearing.
    - 🔵 **Primary**: Nexus Panel binary upgrades & Xray-Core kernel upgrades.
    - ⚪ **Info**: General transactional confirmations.
  - **Full Keyboard Accessibility**: Auto-focus on primary action, `Escape` key to safely dismiss/cancel, `Enter` key to confirm.
  - **Click-outside & Backdrop Blur**: Darkened frosted backdrop prevents accidental interactions while retaining full context.
